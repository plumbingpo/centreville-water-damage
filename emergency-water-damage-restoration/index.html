Maintenance Tips for Emergency Water Damage Restoration

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season and consider a battery backup.
  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Every member of the household should know where the main water valve is and how it closes.
  • Being able to find the electrical panel quickly means wet areas can be de-energized without risk.
  • Aging supply lines to toilets, faucets and washing machines should be swapped out before one fails.

When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Centreville

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • An outlet, a light fixture or the ceiling above has water dripping out of it.
  • Wet carpet, wood flooring or wallboard has stayed saturated longer than a few hours.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • The water is from a sewer backup or looks dark and smells bad.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • You have no way to locate or get to the shutoff while water continues to pour.
